About

Hello — we're Everlight.

We are a small studio of two, and we have spent the last decade photographing weddings in barns, ballrooms, temples, town halls and back gardens across four continents. Big celebrations and quiet ones. We have never yet found a wedding that wasn't worth photographing properly.

Our approach is simple: we stay out of the way. No stiff group shots that run past sunset, no directing you through a moment that was already happening on its own. We watch, we wait, and we photograph what is actually there — which, on a wedding day, is usually more than enough.

Questions

Good to know

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples reach out nine to eighteen months ahead, and peak Saturdays tend to go first. That said, we keep a few dates open each season for shorter notice — if your date is close, ask anyway.

Do you travel for weddings?

Gladly, and often. Travel within the country is included in every collection. For destination weddings we quote flights and accommodation transparently at cost — no markup, no surprise line items.

When will we see our photographs?

A preview of around thirty images lands within a week. The full gallery follows in six to eight weeks, delivered privately online in high resolution with a print release, so you can print whatever you like, wherever you like.

Do you photograph cultural and multi-day weddings?

Yes, regularly — mehndi, haldi, sangeet, nikkah, church and civil ceremonies, and celebrations that run across several days and venues. We will ask plenty of questions beforehand so we know what matters and where to stand.

What if we hate having our picture taken?

Almost everyone says this, and it is almost never true by the end of the day. We work quietly and give light direction only when it genuinely helps. Couples who dread the camera usually come away with our favourite galleries.

How do we secure our date?

A signed agreement and a 25% retainer hold the date; the balance is due two weeks before the wedding. We only take one wedding per weekend, so the date is properly yours once it's booked.
